Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Account Within The App

The fastest way to permanently delete your account and all associated health data is directly within the OneHealth app:

  • 1. Open the OneHealth app and log in.
  • 2. Navigate to the Settings tab.
  • 3. Tap on Account Settings.
  • 4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone and tap Delete Permanently.

Request Deletion via Web

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request that we manually wipe your data. Please send an email to our support team from the email address associated with your account.

To: ikhlasah06@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Note: For security purposes, we can only process data deletion requests sent from the email address verified and linked to your OneHealth account. Once processed, all health profiles, records, and authentication data are permanently wiped from our secure database and cannot be recovered.
