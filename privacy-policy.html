Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

OneHealth ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information and health data are collected, used, and disclosed by OneHealth.

2. Data Collection

We collect the following types of information when you use our app:

  • Identity Information: Name, email address, and authentication data.
  • Health & Medical Data: Medications, medical conditions, allergies, vaccines, medical appointments, hospital visits, procedures, dental records, vision records, and insurance details.
  • Device Information: We may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic data to improve app stability.

3. Use of Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide the core functionality of the OneHealth app. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is securely stored and transmitted using industry-standard encryption via Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. We utilize strict security rules to ensure that only authenticated users can access their own data.

5. Google Play Store Health App Compliance

OneHealth operates in strict compliance with the Google Play Store's Health App policies:

  • We do not share your health data with ad networks, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • Health data is only accessed and processed to fulfill the app's declared functionalities.

6. Account Deletion

You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated health data at any time from within the app's settings menu, or by visiting our Account Deletion page.
