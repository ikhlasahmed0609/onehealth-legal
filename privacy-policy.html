Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to OneHealth. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your highly sensitive health information. This Comprehensive Privacy Policy explains in detail how your personal identity and medical data are collected, utilized, secured, and managed when you use the OneHealth mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with a comprehensive medical record-keeping experience, we collect specific categories of information:

  • Identity Information: Your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and Email Address. This is required to create a secure account.
  • Comprehensive Health Data: Information you choose to input into the app, which may include:
    • Current and past medical conditions
    • Medications, dosages, and schedules
    • Allergies (food, drug, environmental)
    • Vaccination and immunization history
    • Surgical procedures and hospital visits
    • Dental and Vision records
    • Healthcare provider details and appointments
    • Health insurance policy information
  • Device Permissions: We may request access to push notifications to provide you with app updates. We do not track your background location.

3. How We Use Your Data

We strictly limit the use of your data to the following core purposes:

  • Core Functionality: To securely store, organize, and display your health records to you across your devices.
  • Account Security: To authenticate your logins and protect your account from unauthorized access.
  • App Improvement: To monitor crash logs and identify software bugs (using anonymized, non-health data).

Strict Prohibition on Data Sales: We explicitly declare that we do NOT sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal or health data to any third parties, advertising networks, data brokers, or pharmaceutical companies under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

OneHealth operates on a secure cloud infrastructure provided by Google. We utilize Google Firebase Authentication for secure login handling and Firebase Cloud Firestore for encrypted database storage. These services comply with stringent international security standards (ISO 27001, SOC 1, SOC 2, SOC 3).

5. Data Security & Encryption

We take the security of your health data very seriously. All data transmitted between the OneHealth app and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS (Transport Layer Security). Furthermore, your data is encrypted at rest on Google Cloud servers. We implement strict Firebase Security Rules to guarantee that only your successfully authenticated account can read or write your specific health profile data.

6. Google Play Store Health App Compliance

OneHealth operates in strict compliance with the Google Play Store's Health Apps policy. We affirm that:

  • We clearly disclose our data collection practices in this policy.
  • Health data is only accessed to fulfill the app's declared core functionalities.
  • We do not utilize any APIs or SDKs that improperly monetize health data.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We explicitly enforce an age requirement during the sign-up process. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 18. If a parent or guardian creates a health profile on behalf of a minor child within their own account, they assume full responsibility for that data.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may at any time:

  • Access and Modify: Edit or delete individual medical records directly within the app.
  • Complete Deletion: Permanently wipe your entire account and all associated health data. This can be done via the app (Settings > Account Settings > Delete Permanently) or by visiting our Account Deletion page. When deleted, all records are immediately and irreversibly destroyed from our active servers.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app functionality. We will notify you of any significant changes via the email address associated with your account or through an in-app alert.
